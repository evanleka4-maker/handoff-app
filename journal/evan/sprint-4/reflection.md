## Hypothesis → Build → Measure → Learn
<!-- Evan's account. Separate what he observed from what he interpreted. -->

**Nov 19, 2026, what I set out to test and what I did:** I Talked to Enclave 425 and Bulldog Crossing and asked them if they would be interested in a demo of Handoff to gauge their interest. Neither of them responded with interest of my demo, thus leading me to believe they wouldn’t pay for it because they weren’t interested in the first place. Perhaps I need to work on my “selling” skills. Since demo day wasn’t public, there was no information to use.

**Nov 19, 2026, what I observed:** At Enclave 425 I mentioned that I was working on Handoff after asking what software they use and the challenges that they face with August. When I mentioned that, it seemed like they felt like I was there to sell them a product or solicit and while they were kind, they shot me down, emphasizing that they don’t make the decisions anyway and seemed like they wanted the conversation to end. At Bulldog Crossing, similar results. After asking about their setup and issues, I mentioned Handoff and the property manager was suddenly disengaged and made an effort to end the conversation mentioning how they had to take care of something and they were busy. Neither showed interest in what I was there to show.

**Nov 19, 2026, what I think it means:** Based on these two conversations, I think that if I were to pitch a demo to gauge interest to operators, I need to improve on my soft skills related to sales even if I’m not necessarily selling anything right away. Perhaps I need to do a better job of convincing operators they need what I have. Other than that, I’m not sure what to do.
